From Camp to Campus

Social investment in refugees and newcomers through higher education · FWO Strategic Basic Research (S003124N)

As a researcher at HIVA I developed the project's conceptual framework for migrant integration through social investment in education, and a new measure of education investment effort across European countries. This work was presented at the WeLaR final conference (2025) and at the Social Investment Working Group of the European University Institute (2026).

2019–2024

Re-InVEST.be

Investing in basic rights, capabilities and anti-poverty policies in Belgium · BELSPO BRAIN-be 2.0

I worked on the socio-economic analysis of social protection and health. This included long-term poverty dynamics in Belgium (2008–2017) on linked administrative data, survival analysis of poverty entry, exit and re-entry, the effect of social benefit generosity on households' economic resilience, and the relation between financial resources, housing, social protection and health (EU-SILC 2020). I am first author of two project reports and co-author of the final report to BELSPO.
