Ongoing · 2023–2027

From Camp to Campus

Social investment in refugees and newcomers through higher education · Research Foundation Flanders (FWO), Strategic Basic Research (SBO)

The project studies how investing in the higher education of refugees and newcomers can support their integration, with benefits for them and for Flemish society. My role is to supervise the work of PhD researcher Pol Barbé.

Project website

Ongoing · 2024–2026

HEQUITY

Building Higher Education Quality Inclusion Pathways · European Commission, Erasmus+ (KA220-HED)

A partnership of five organisations from Belgium, Turkey, Italy and Latvia that develops tools and training to help universities include migrant and refugee students. KU Leuven leads the capacity-building work package. Activities include a toolkit development meeting in Padova (June 2026) and an international training session at Sakarya University, Turkey (September 2026).

Completed · 2021–2025

EfficientJustice

Efficient Justice for All: Improving court efficiency through EU benchmarking · COST Action CA20131, European Cooperation in Science and Technology (COST)

As a member of this European research network, I contributed to work on measuring and benchmarking the efficiency of courts across EU countries.

Completed · 2019–2024

Re-InVEST.be

Investing in basic rights, capabilities and anti-poverty policies in Belgium · Belgian Science Policy Office (BELSPO), BRAIN-be 2.0

I joined in 2023 and worked on the socio-economic analysis of social protection and health: long-term poverty dynamics in Belgium (2008–2017) on linked administrative data, survival analysis of poverty entry, exit and re-entry, the effect of social benefit generosity on households' economic resilience, and the relation between financial resources, housing, social protection and health (EU-SILC 2020). I am first author of two project reports and co-author of the final report to BELSPO.
