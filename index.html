Anna
Mergoni

I am a Senior Researcher at HIVA – Research Institute for Work and Society, KU Leuven, in the research group Poverty and Migration. I hold a PhD in Economics from KU Leuven.

My research looks at how public policies, from social protection to education, shape people's opportunities, and whether they use the resources invested in them well. I study long-term poverty in Belgium with large linked administrative datasets (tax records, Demobel, Census), and education and social investment across Europe with EU-SILC, Eurostat and international assessments (PISA, TIMSS, PIRLS).

I combine causal inference, longitudinal and survival analysis, multilevel and structural equation models, composite indicators, and non-parametric efficiency analysis. I wrote and maintain the R package rcDEA.

I enjoy sharing methods and findings with different audiences. I teach quantitative methods to master and PhD students at KU Leuven and in the FLAMES training network, give invited lectures on AI in research, and present my work to policymakers and practitioners, including at the European Parliament and Cedefop.
