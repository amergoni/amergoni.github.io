Research

My research covers poverty and social protection, the economics of education, and the measurement of public sector performance. I combine causal inference with non-parametric efficiency methods to ask not only whether policies work, but whether they use resources well.

Research focus Publications Book chapters Working papers Reports Software

Research focus

Poverty & social protection

Long-term poverty dynamics in Belgium using linked administrative data (tax register, Demobel, Census); poverty entry, exit and re-entry; how the generosity of social benefits shapes households' economic resilience; and links between income, housing and health.

Education policy

Equal opportunity policies, school funding, ICT in schools, principals' management practices, tracking, and gender differences in achievement, using administrative data and international assessments (PISA, TIMSS, PIRLS). More recently, social investment in the education of refugees and newcomers.

Efficiency & evaluation methods

Methods that combine causal inference with non-parametric frontier estimation (DEA, FDH, conditional and robust estimators), composite indicators and Monte Carlo simulation, implemented in the R package rcDEA. Try the interactive explainer.
